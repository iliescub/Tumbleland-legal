Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Data Not CollectedNo adsNo chatWorks offline

Tumbleland is a single-player game that works completely offline. It does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts, no chat, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

Your game progress, settings and the courses you build are saved only on this device, in the app's own storage. They are included in the device's normal backups (for example iCloud Backup, if you use it), which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Deleting the app deletes all of its data. A parent can also reset progress or delete the built courses in the Parent Corner.

The optional Parent Corner PIN is stored on this device as a one-way scrambled code (a hash), never as the PIN itself.
